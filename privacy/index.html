Neon Plates — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Neon Plates is a car database and garage tracker made by Olex Apps. This policy explains what the app does with information about you. If you have a question, write to olexapps.dev@gmail.com.

In short

The current version of Neon Plates does not collect personal data, does not send data about you to us or to anyone else, and has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics.

What stays on your device

The cars you mark as owned or wanted and the language you pick are saved in the app's own storage on your phone. They never leave the device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Permissions

The app does not ask for access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files. The car list is built into the app, so it works without an internet connection.

App stores

When you download Neon Plates, Google Play or the Apple App Store processes data under its own privacy policy. The stores may share aggregated statistics and crash reports with us; these do not identify you.

Children

Neon Plates is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

Later versions may add advertising, in-app purchases and anonymous usage statistics. Before such a version is released, this page will describe exactly what is collected, by whom and why, and the date at the top will change.

Not affiliated

Neon Plates is an unofficial fan app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.
